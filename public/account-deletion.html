WristoPrivacy policy

Request deletion of your Wristo account

Wristo is operated by WuKong OÜ. You can request deletion of your Wristo account and associated personal data without installing or signing in to the app.

Send your request

Email support@wristo.io from the email address associated with your Wristo account, with the subject Wristo account deletion. If you cannot access that email address, contact the same support address for help verifying ownership.

Open email app

Opening your email app does not submit a request. Review and send the email. Do not include passwords, sign-in codes, or watch activation codes.

What the request covers

Your account is shared across Wristo for Android, iOS and the web. The request covers the account profile, linked sign-in identities and associated personal data. Tell support if you also have an active subscription so they can help you with cancellation. Deleting an account or uninstalling an app does not by itself cancel a subscription with its billing provider.

What happens next

Support will verify ownership and explain the deletion process, expected completion date, and any records that need to be retained for security, fraud prevention or legal obligations, including the reason and retention period. Contact support at any time to follow up on your request.

Signing out or uninstalling Wristo does not delete your server account. Local favorites and the selected Garmin model can be removed using Android Settings → Apps → Wristo → Storage → Clear storage.
